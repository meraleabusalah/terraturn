How TerraTurn Works

Giving an unwanted item a second life doesn't have to be complicated. TerraTurn helps you discover practical ways to reduce waste, one item at a time.

01

Upload or Take a Photo

Choose a photo of an unwanted item from your device, or use your camera to take a picture. Start with an item you want to repair, reuse, donate, or recycle.

02

Identify Your Item

TerraTurn uses image recognition to predict what your item is. Review the result and use it to explore suitable next steps.

03

Choose a Better Option

Explore four possibilities: repair the item, reuse it for another purpose, rehome it with someone who needs it, or recycle it where accepted.

04

Find Places Near You

For repair, rehoming, and recycling, select your UAE emirate and search for relevant places using the location finder.

05

Take Action

Follow the suggested steps and choose an option that suits your item. Always check local requirements, especially for batteries and electronic waste.

06

Track Your Impact

Mark actions as completed to build your personal record and view illustrative estimates of the environmental impact of your choices.
